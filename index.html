Your first visit is a Brow Makeover.

Every new client starts here. One appointment covers the whole ritual, so your brows are designed for your face from the start.

  1. Consult

    We talk through the look you want and what suits your features.

  2. Map

    Your brows are measured and mapped to the balance of your face.

  3. Trim

    Long hairs are trimmed so the shape sits neatly.

  4. Shape

    Waxed to the mapped shape.

  5. Tint

    Colour matched from our extensive range of tints.
