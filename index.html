Every new client starts with a Brow Makeover.

Before you book.

Our appointment policy, so there are no surprises.

Cancellations and no-shows

We need at least 24 hours' notice if you can't make it. Cancelling with less than 24 hours' notice is charged at 50% of the booking, and no-shows at 100%. If it's out of trading hours or we can't answer, please leave a message.

Rescheduling on the day

Moving your appointment to a later time on the same day may be charged as a late cancellation, as we have less time to fill your spot.

Changing your service

Let us know 24 hours before your appointment if you need to change the services you're booked for, to avoid being charged in full for the original booking.

Reminders

Please write your appointment down rather than relying on our text or email reminders.
